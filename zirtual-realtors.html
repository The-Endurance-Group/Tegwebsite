Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The 30-minute call is free and carries no obligation. We talk about what you want AI to do. The $998 only applies if you then decide you want us to build it.

What does the $998 cover?

A fixed price for everything we agree on the first call, as long as it fits our scope. That includes building it, installing it, walking you through it and a follow-up call about a week later. You pay $499 on signing and $499 on delivery.

Will it send messages or post for me?

No. Everything is a draft until you approve it. You review replies, follow-ups and listing content before anything is sent or published, and you stay responsible for the final wording, including fair housing language.

What does the daily brief need access to?

A brief that reads iMessage and Notes needs access to your computer. We configure that with you on the install call, and you choose what it can see. Email and calendar connect through the tools you already use.

Does this replace my Zirtual assistant?

No. A real person from our team is on every call, and the setup is designed to work alongside your Zirtual assistant. Your assistant keeps doing what they do, with a clearer brief and drafts already waiting.

What is not included?

Custom software, scrapers, building new integrations and website migrations are out of scope. We quote those separately, and we tell you on the free call if something you want falls into that group.
