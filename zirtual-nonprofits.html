Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The 30-minute call is free and carries no obligation. We talk about what you want AI to do. The $998 only applies if you then decide you want us to build it.

What does the $998 cover?

A fixed price for everything we agree on the first call, as long as it fits our scope. That includes building it, installing it, walking you through it and a follow-up call about a week later. You pay $499 on signing and $499 on delivery.

Which budget line should the $998 come from?

It depends on the build and on your funders' rules. Grant and donor builds usually fit Development/Fundraising. Event and newsletter builds usually fit Marketing and Communications. Task briefs fit Operations/Administration, and reminders fit Finance and Compliance. Confirm coding with your finance lead or auditor.

Is this a subscription?

No. $998 is a one-time fixed price with no monthly fee to us. You will pay your own AI plan and any other tools directly to those providers, and we do not mark those costs up.

Does this replace my Zirtual assistant?

No. A real person from our team is on every call, and the setup is designed to work alongside your Zirtual assistant. Your assistant keeps doing what they do, with better drafts and briefs to work from.

What is not included?

Custom software, scrapers, building new integrations and website migrations are out of scope. We quote those separately, and we tell you on the free call if something you want falls into that group.
