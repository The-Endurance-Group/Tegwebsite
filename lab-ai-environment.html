What we can build

Six ways your AI environment can work for you

We choose the pieces that fit your practice in session one. Nobody gets all six.

Connected systems

We link your AI to the tools you already use, through their official connectors where they exist. That can include Kajabi, Canva, Meta Ads, Descript, Outlook email and calendar, Google Workspace and WordPress.

For example: ask for a first draft of this week's newsletter and have it appear ready in your design tool.

Skills in your voice

Reusable instructions and prompt libraries written in your brand voice, so the first draft already sounds like you.

For example: a one-click "turn this talk into a short email series" skill.

Scheduled routines

Jobs that run on a timer without you asking. You wake up to the result.

For example: a daily summary of your inbox, calendar and sales, emailed to you at 7am.

Projects with your content loaded

A dedicated workspace that already holds your programs, FAQs, style guide and past material, so you never re-explain yourself.

For example: a "My Program" project that answers questions using your own course notes.

Dashboards for your data

One clear page for the numbers you check most. Membership, sales, ad performance and project status.

For example: a single view of this month's sign-ups, ad spend and open tasks.

Review-ready content checks

A check that reads your draft and flags items for you to look at before you publish. It is a second pair of eyes, not a sign-off.

For example: a flag on wording that may need a second look, with a note on why.

Questions, answered

Do I need to be technical?

No. You bring your goals and your day-to-day. We handle the setup and explain every step in plain language.

What do I need to bring?

Your logins for the tools you want connected, a few examples of your content or brand voice, and a rough list of the tasks that eat your time. We send a short checklist before session one.

Which AI plan do I need?

We will confirm this in scoping, because it depends on what you want to build. Some connections and scheduled routines need a paid plan. You buy your plan directly from the provider and it is not part of our fee.

Is my data private?

We set the environment up under your own accounts, so your information stays with you and not with us. We help you choose sensible settings. We recommend you do not put identifiable patient information into any tool unless you have checked it is appropriate for that use. Always review each provider's own privacy terms.

Is this compliant?

We cannot promise that, and no tool can. The checks we build can help flag content for your review. Outputs are drafts. You remain responsible for reviewing everything before it is published. TEG does not provide legal or regulatory advice. For questions about Ahpra or TGA rules, speak with your professional body or advisor.

What happens after session 3?

Your environment is yours to keep and use. If you want more built later, such as another dashboard or routine, we can scope that separately. There is no obligation.

Can you build a dashboard only?

Often, yes. Tell us in your enquiry what you want to see and we will let you know the best fit and the fee before you commit.

How does this work with my VA?

It works alongside your VA. The AI layer handles drafting, summaries and routines. Your VA keeps doing the execution, with better inputs and less repeat work. We are happy to show your VA how it works if you would like.
