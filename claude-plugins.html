Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are these Claude plugins really free?

Yes. The plugins and the 30-minute setup session are free, with no contract and no obligation. They are yours to keep and use after the call.

Why do you set it up on a call instead of just posting a download?

A plugin is only useful once it is installed, connected to the right tools, and tried on your real work. On the call we send you the plugin, install it with you, connect anything it needs, and run it on one of your actual tasks, so you leave with it working instead of a file sitting in your downloads folder.

What do I need to use a Claude plugin?

A Claude account on a plan that supports plugins, plus any connector a plugin depends on, such as HubSpot for the HubSpot CRM Toolkit. Most of these plugins also use Claude's web research. We check your setup at the start of the session.

Do the plugins send my data to The Endurance Group?

No. These plugins are instructions that run inside your own Claude account. They do not send anything to TEG, and any connected tool keeps its own terms and permissions.

Can you customize a plugin for our team?

Yes. Every plugin here started as a workflow we built for a real team. If you want one adapted to your process, your tools, or your industry, bring it up on the call.
