System integrations. Automated workflows. Custom Claude applications. Setup and training. Four things, done well, by a certified team that owns the outcome.

What we build

Four things, done well.

System Integrations

  • Connect Claude to your CRM, email, Slack, and document tools — no more copy-pasting between apps
  • Build MCP servers that reach custom applications, proprietary databases, and legacy systems standard tools can't touch

Automated Workflows

  • Build scheduled pipelines that monitor, route, and act on data across your systems — no manual moving required
  • Automate the repetitive work: lead qualification, reconciliation, alerts, follow-ups — so your team focuses on what actually needs them

Custom Claude Applications

  • Build purpose-built Claude tools for your team's specific workflows — internal tools, client-facing products, or back-office systems
  • TEG scopes the build, writes the code, and hands you a working application with your team trained to run it

Claude Set Up and Training

  • Configure Claude for your environment — Projects, Skills, Connectors, Code, and the specific workflows your people actually run
  • Train your team hands-on so they're doing real work on day one, not just experimenting with prompts
