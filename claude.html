How it starts

From first call to live workflows in 14 days.

We've done this with ops teams at Offerpad, Realty ONE Group, and others. Here's how we start with yours.

1

Discovery call: map where your team's time goes

A 30–45 minute call. We learn how your team uses Claude today, identify your five highest-value workflows, and agree on the build plan before we touch anything.

2

We build and configure — no technical lift from your team

We set up your org, build your Skills, connect your tools via MCP, and populate your Projects and knowledge files. Everything goes live in 14 days. Your team keeps working while we build.

3

Handoff: your team knows how to build, not just use

We walk through everything we built, train your team on how to extend it themselves, and stay available as Claude evolves. They leave able to create new Skills and Cowork routines on their own.
