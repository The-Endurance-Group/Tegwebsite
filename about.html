About
"Through endurance we conquer." - Ernest Shackleton

We've been doing this work since 1998. The tools have changed. The principle hasn't.

At TEG, we help people be people, and build technology to do everything else. Judgment, care, and connection are the human parts of work we protect. Repetitive, manual work is what we automate.

The Endurance · Frank Hurley, 1915 · Public domain

The story behind the name

Shackleton didn't reach the pole. He brought every person home.

In 1914, Shackleton's ship became trapped in Antarctic ice and was crushed. The mission failed. What followed was one of the greatest survival stories ever recorded: all 27 crew members made it home after nearly two years stranded at the bottom of the world. Jim Sullivan named this company after that principle in 1998. The Endurance Group has been in business for over 25 years, through market cycles, technology shifts, and the emergence of AI. We started in real estate, spent two decades running outsourced B2B sales and marketing, and now implement Claude for B2B teams.
