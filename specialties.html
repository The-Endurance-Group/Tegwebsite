Where we apply Claude

The ops work we know best. Now handled by Claude.

Three specialties: revenue operations, finance operations, and service operations. We implement Claude across all three, backed by 20+ years of running this work from the inside, so we know exactly where Claude makes the biggest difference.

Functional Specialties
Our deepest specialty

Revenue operations (RevOps)

Lead qualification, outreach, follow-up, pipeline management, CRM hygiene. TEG ran this work inside client teams for over 20 years, not advising on it but doing it. That's why RevOps is where we have the clearest instincts and the strongest proof.

Finance operations (FinOps)

Invoice processing, AP/AR reconciliation, expense workflows, vendor management, budget reporting. We know where finance teams are still doing work a system should have owned and we build the automation that handles it.

For Offerpad, we built back-office automations that matched utility and vendor invoices against property records automatically, eliminating hours of manual reconciliation across multiple departments.

Service operations

Dispatch, scheduling, job completion workflows, billing sync. For companies where a completed job triggers a chain of updates across multiple systems, we build the automation that runs that chain so no one reconciles it by hand at the end of the week.

For a field service company, we automated the job completion chain: every closed job triggers the invoice, time log, customer record update, and accounting entry, with no manual reconciliation at week's end.

These are the three we go deepest in. We build across other ops functions too: HR, customer success, IT, business ops. If the work is manual, bring it.
