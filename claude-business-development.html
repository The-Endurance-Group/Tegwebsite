Business development, built around relationships

Most outbound fails because it starts cold.

Your next clients may already be connected to people you know. We help find those paths and open the conversation.

We start with your team’s relationships, reputation and networks. Then we use Claude to research accounts, identify promising introductions and write to each prospect as an individual. Senior people still open the doors. AI helps bring that level of preparation to more of your target accounts.

This is the same Claude capability we build for clients, run for you by TEG. We handle the research, campaigns and follow-up so your people can focus on relationships and conversations.

How it works

Five steps from target list to conversations.

  1. 01

    Define your ideal customer

    We build your ideal customer profile from your best clients and won deals. Together, we identify the characteristics that make an account worth pursuing.

  2. 02

    Find the accounts and people

    We identify target companies and decision-makers, ranked by fit and timing, so the team spends its attention on relevant opportunities.

  3. 03

    Map your warm paths

    We look for former colleagues, shared employers and mutual connections. For each target, we assess the strongest available route into a conversation.

  4. 04

    Write to each prospect personally

    Claude helps write an individual message based on the person’s role, company news and relevant shared connections. TEG runs email and LinkedIn outreach from your accounts, in your voice.

  5. 05

    Connect the work to your systems

    We connect Claude with your CRM and Gmail or Microsoft 365, with the access agreed for your engagement. Research, drafts and follow-ups belong where your team already works.
