Real Estate

Renovation & Portfolio Ops

Vendor invoice and photo reconciliation for a national iBuyer's reno team — invoice line items matched against property records by address, flagged before approval.
B2B Technology & SaaS

Contract & Compliance Ops

Incoming agreements, vendor contracts, and compliance documents need to clear a checklist before they go to legal or finance. Manual review at scale creates backlogs and gaps.

Automated document intake checking for required fields, missing signatures, and non-standard terms — flagged before they reach the review queue.
Professional Services

Deliverable & Filing Ops

Deadlines are fixed, and the cost of a missed filing or incomplete submission lands on the ops person who was supposed to check. Finding gaps after the fact is the worst version of this problem.

Pre-deadline completeness scans that surface what's missing and route it to the responsible person with enough lead time to fix it.
