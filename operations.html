Every engagement starts the same way. What comes next depends on you.

1

Discovery call: we learn your situation

A 30–45 minute call. We understand where your team is with Claude, what's working, what isn't, and what would move the needle most. No assumptions about what you need going in.

We recommend the right service

Sometimes that's a full org buildout. Sometimes it's a single Skill or MCP connection. Sometimes it's a training session for your team. We tell you what we think will actually help, not what's most expensive.
