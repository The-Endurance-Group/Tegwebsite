1. Website visitor identification

Turn identified website visitors into a ranked list for an owner.

Why it fits

Buyers research on the company website before they ever reply to an email. The team already has a visitor-identification tool, but the list is never reviewed.

Where it comes from

The company website, through the visitor-identification tool the team already pays for.

Who to pursue

  • Companies with 50 to 200 employees
  • Visitors who viewed pricing or a case study
  • Finance and accounting companies

Who to skip

  • Companies under 20 or over 500 employees
  • Existing customers, vendors and staff
  • Single-page bounces

Messaging angle

Point to the exact page they read, then offer a short walkthrough of how teams close the books faster.

Suggested schedule: Weekdays at 3am ET

Your plan

  1. Pull newly identified visitors from the visitor-identification tool
  2. Check company fit against the target customer profile
  3. Screen for the 50 to 200 employee range
  4. Check the CRM and outreach tool and skip existing contacts
  5. Enrich only the visitors who pass screening
  6. Rank by pages viewed and assign an owner
  7. Queue the leads paused for approval

Built-in safeguards

  • Dedupes against your CRM and outreach tools first
  • Skips existing contacts, customers and anyone already in a sequence
  • Enriches only the leads that pass screening
  • Stops short of sending anything
  • Queues leads paused for your approval

  • Check fit and size
  • Route to an owner

In the morning: Ranked visitors, why each fits and the owner assigned.

2. Hiring signal

Find companies posting roles that reveal the problem the product solves.

Why it fits

A company that posts a role for manual reconciliation work has already decided the problem is worth paying for. The product replaces part of that role.

Where it comes from

Public job boards and company career pages.

Who to pursue

  • Accounting or finance operations roles at mid-sized firms
  • Firms with no large in-house finance technology team

Who to skip

  • Staffing agencies reposting the same role
  • Enterprise companies with a large finance technology team

Messaging angle

Mention the role they posted and show how a team handles the same work before the new hire starts.

Suggested schedule: Weekdays at 2am ET

Your plan

  1. Search job boards for reconciliation and close-process roles
  2. Screen each company against the target customer profile
  3. Check the CRM and outreach tool and skip existing contacts
  4. Find the person the new hire would report to
  5. Enrich only that qualified contact
  6. Draft a short note based on the role being hired
  7. Queue the lead paused for approval

Built-in safeguards

  • Dedupes against your CRM and outreach tools first
  • Skips existing contacts, customers and anyone already in a sequence
  • Enriches only the leads that pass screening
  • Stops short of sending anything
  • Queues leads paused for your approval

  • Find the hiring manager
  • Draft a note

3. Email engagement

Follow up with contacts who click or repeatedly open one-to-one emails.

Why it fits

The team already sends one-to-one emails but follows up by memory. Clicks and repeat opens show who is paying attention right now.

Where it comes from

Open and click activity from the team’s email or outreach tool.

Who to pursue

  • Contacts who clicked a link
  • Contacts who opened the same email several times

Who to skip

  • Automated opens from mail scanners
  • Contacts who already replied or booked a call

Messaging angle

Reference the link they clicked and offer one specific next step, such as a sample close checklist.

Suggested schedule: Weekdays at 6am ET

Your plan

  1. Pull yesterday’s clicks and repeat opens on one-to-one emails
  2. Remove scanner activity and contacts who already replied
  3. Check the CRM for open deals and skip anyone in an active conversation
  4. Decide the next step for each remaining contact
  5. Draft a short follow-up for each contact worth pursuing
  6. Rank by recency and strength of engagement
  7. Queue the drafts paused for approval

  • Dedupes against your CRM and outreach tools first
  • Skips existing contacts, customers and anyone already in a sequence
  • Enriches only the leads that pass screening
  • Stops short of sending anything
  • Queues leads paused for your approval
