How Managed Claude works

An ongoing relationship, not a handoff.

1

We learn how your business operates

We start with your workflows, not AI features. Where does your team spend time? What's repetitive? What's manual? Where are employees moving information between systems?

2

We build the first high-value use cases

Skills, MCPs, integrations, workflows, training, or applications — whatever produces the most value first.

3

We keep going

The first implementation isn't the end of the engagement. Each month TEG maintains what exists, solves problems, identifies new opportunities, and builds the next things that make your team faster.

No lock-in

Everything We Build Stays Yours.

No proprietary platform you have to adopt. Whenever possible, TEG builds directly inside the tools your company already uses — Claude, your CRM, your documents, and your internal systems.

  • Your integrations are yours.
  • Your workflows are yours.
  • Your code is yours.
  • Your data remains yours.
