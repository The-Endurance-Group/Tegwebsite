What is Managed Claude?

TEG is your ongoing Claude team. We maintain and improve existing workflows, support and train employees, update documentation, review value and assess new opportunities.

What does the monthly fee cover?

The agreed environment and service scope at launch. Managed Claude starts at $1,500/month, billed monthly. New workflows and applications are separately scoped and value-priced before implementation. Clients pay Claude subscriptions, third-party software and API charges separately.

Can the monthly fee change?

As workflows, integrations, teams or support requirements grow, we may recommend adjusting the monthly fee. Any change is agreed with you before it takes effect. We confirm service scope, billing start date, term and cancellation provisions in your agreement before you commit.

Who owns what TEG builds?

You own the workflows, Skills, integrations and code we build. Whenever possible, we work inside the tools your company already uses.
