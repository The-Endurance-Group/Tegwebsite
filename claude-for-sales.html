Frequently asked questions

What can Claude automate for a sales team?

Common starting points are CRM updates from approved notes and emails, proposal drafts, lead research and qualification, pipeline monitoring, follow-up drafts and sales reporting. The free AI Value Assessment identifies which are worth building for your team.

Does Claude work with HubSpot and Salesforce?

Claude can connect to CRMs such as HubSpot and Salesforce. We confirm what is possible for your specific setup during the assessment, before agreeing a scope and price.

Will Claude send messages or change records on its own?

Not without your approval. Customer-facing messages, financial commitments and important record changes are reviewed and approved by your team.

How much does it cost?

The AI Value Assessment is free. Implementation is a fixed price based on expected value and scope. Managed Claude, our standard ongoing service, starts at $1,500/month. New builds are separately scoped and priced.
