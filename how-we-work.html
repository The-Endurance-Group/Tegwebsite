Why we stay involved

As employees learn AI, new needs and opportunities emerge. Claude changes fast. Your workflows need ongoing assessment, refinement and improvement.

AI can create a competitive advantage. Getting it wrong can create costly mistakes. TEG helps your team keep improving while reviewing how AI is used.

  • Maintenance
  • Technology updates
  • Team support & training
  • Documentation
  • New opportunity assessments
  • Monthly value reviews

Your team learns. Claude gets better.

For example, after we launch a proposal workflow:

Your team spots a problem

Claude drafts proposals, but employees keep correcting the same product details.

We improve the workflow

We update Claude’s reference material, test the drafts and show your team what changed.

We maintain TEG-built workflows, keep technology and documentation current, and support and train your team.
