    A few common questions

    Is the playbook free?

    Yes, with no obligation. Pricing for building the flows is shared on a call.

    What is an intent signal?

    A sign that a company may need what you sell, like engaging with a vendor, visiting your site, or hiring for the problem you solve. It suggests an opportunity worth a look, not a guaranteed sale.

    Will anything be sent automatically?

    No. Leads are queued paused until you approve them.
