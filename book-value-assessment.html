    A few common questions

    Is it free, and am I obligated to proceed?

    Yes, it is free. The questionnaire and your AI Value Assessment cost nothing, and your first meeting-based assessment is free for one team, with up to five hours of meetings and research. There is no obligation to proceed with a build. If we don’t find a worthwhile build, we’ll tell you.

    What happens after I see my report?

    You can book a time with us right below your report. We choose the team with the biggest opportunity, validate the estimates with the people who do the work, and send an AI Value Report within one week after the assessment is complete.

    Do I need Claude already?

    No. We identify any Claude setup or software requirements during the assessment.

    How accurate are the numbers in the instant assessment?

    They are initial estimates based on your answers, not a finished analysis. The meeting-based assessment replaces them with figures validated against your real workflows and gives you fixed implementation prices.

    What access or data do you need?

    No passwords or system access are needed for the questionnaire or the first call. If we proceed, we agree what access and data handling the scope requires.

    Are software costs included?

    No. Clients pay Claude subscriptions, third-party software and API charges separately from TEG fees. We confirm the requirements and costs before work begins.
