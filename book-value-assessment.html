A few common questions

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. After the introductory call, your free assessment covers one team or department and includes up to five hours in total of meetings and research to create the report. There is no obligation to build every opportunity we identify.

What should I bring to the call?

Bring a team or department you have in mind, such as sales or finance, and a short overview of what you’d like to improve. We use this call to choose where to focus; detailed meetings and research come afterward.

Do I need Claude already?

No. We can start with how your team works today and identify any Claude setup or software requirements as part of the assessment.

What happens after the assessment?

Within one week after the assessment is complete, you receive an AI Value Report: prioritized builds, what each would do, expected 12-month economic value, and a fixed implementation price. You choose which opportunities to pursue.

What if there isn’t a worthwhile build?

We’ll tell you. The assessment is about finding measurable value, and there is no obligation to proceed with an implementation.

Are projected values guaranteed savings?

No. Estimates use agreed inputs and assumptions. Time freed is capacity value, avoided hiring depends on a real hiring plan, and new revenue depends on demand and delivery costs. We distinguish these in your report.

Are Claude subscriptions and software costs included?

No. Clients pay Claude subscriptions, third-party software and API charges separately from TEG’s implementation and Managed Claude fees. We identify the requirements and confirm these costs before work begins.

What does Managed Claude cover?

Managed Claude starts at $1,500/month and is the standard ongoing TEG relationship. It covers maintenance of TEG-built workflows, updates, employee support, documentation, training, ongoing assessments and monthly value reviews. New builds are separately scoped and value-priced.

When does Managed Claude billing begin, and what are the terms?

We confirm the monthly service scope, billing start date, term and cancellation provisions in your agreement before you commit. Any fee adjustment as your environment grows is agreed in advance.

Who owns the workflows, and what access do you need?

You own the workflows, Skills, integrations and code we build. For the initial call, start by describing the work; you do not need to share passwords or grant system access. If we proceed, we agree the access and data handling needed for the scope.

How long does implementation take?

Timing depends on the build and your systems. We agree the scope and delivery schedule in a project exhibit before kickoff. Payment is 50% at kickoff and 50% at launch, with a 30-day warranty for reasonable fixes and refinements.
