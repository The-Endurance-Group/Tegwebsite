Fellowship Program

TEG Claude Certification Fellowship

Building a Colombian bench of Claude architects and developers for real client work.

TEG is growing its capacity to deliver Claude-based automation and AI systems for clients, and we're looking to build a dedicated team of Colombian developers to help us do it.

This isn't a general call. I lived in Colombia for two years and taught at Colegio Bolívar in Cali. I've seen firsthand the technical talent coming out of Colombian universities, and I want to build a long-term, dedicated team of young, talented Colombians as part of TEG's delivery bench.

The TEG Claude Certification Fellowship is a paid path into contractor work with TEG — not a one-time exam or a trial with no follow-through. We review your application, cover the cost of your Claude Certified Developer exam, and bring you into our contractor pool for real client project work. This is the start of an ongoing working relationship.

At a glance
  • Exam fee ($125) covered as part of onboarding
  • Paid contractor work on real TEG client projects
  • Direct mentorship from an active Claude development team
  • A recognized certification and portfolio, regardless of what comes next
  • Path to a larger, ongoing role as TEG grows
Requirements

Who we're looking for

We're focused on Colombian developers who are early in their career and serious about building with AI.

  • Currently studying or recently graduated in computer science or a related technical field
  • At least one year of coursework or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Working knowledge of Python or JavaScript
  • Comfortable with REST APIs and JSON
  • Basic familiarity with Git
  • Strong enough English to communicate directly with our English-speaking leadership team
  • Hands-on experience building with Claude or the Anthropic API — apps, Claude Skills, Claude Code extensions, Cowork projects, or any real work in the Anthropic ecosystem
The process

How it works

Apply

Send your CV (showing your major and coursework), a link to your GitHub with any Claude or AI-related projects, and evidence of English proficiency — a TOEFL score, or the school where you studied in English.

Get certified

If your application is a good fit, TEG covers the cost of your Claude Certified Developer exam ($125). We'll also point you to Anthropic's free prep materials so you're ready going in.

Join the delivery team

Once certified, you're eligible for paid contractor work on real TEG client projects. It starts project-based and can grow into a larger, ongoing role as we take on more client work.

Benefits

What you get

Exam fee covered

TEG covers the $125 Claude Certified Developer exam as part of your onboarding — no upfront cost to you.

Real production work

You build actual client automations from the start — not toy projects or training exercises.

Direct mentorship

You work alongside a team that's actively building with Claude for clients — not learning from videos alone.

A credential that travels

The Claude Certified Developer certification is yours to keep, and your portfolio is yours regardless of what comes next.

A path to ongoing work

Project-based work is the start. As TEG takes on more client work, the role grows.

Work with a US team

Direct communication with English-speaking leadership — good experience for any international career path.
