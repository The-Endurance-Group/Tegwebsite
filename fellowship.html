Fellowship Program

TEG Claude Certification Fellowship

A paid path into real Claude client work, with exam fees covered and direct mentorship from an active delivery team.

TEG is building a dedicated team of Claude-certified developers and architects to deliver Claude implementations for our clients.

This isn't a general call for resumes. We're looking for developers who are serious about building with Claude, have already gotten their hands dirty with the Anthropic ecosystem, and want to grow into an ongoing role as TEG expands its client base.

At a glance
  • Exam fees covered
  • Paid contractor work on real TEG client projects
  • Direct mentorship from an active Claude development team
  • A recognized certification and portfolio, regardless of what comes next
  • Path to a larger, ongoing role
Requirements

Who we're looking for

We're looking for developers who are early in their career and serious about building with Claude.

  • Currently studying or recently graduated in computer science or a related technical field
  • At least one year of coursework or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Working knowledge of Python or JavaScript
  • Comfortable with REST APIs and JSON
  • Basic familiarity with Git
  • Strong enough English to communicate directly with our English-speaking leadership team
  • Hands-on experience building with Claude or the Anthropic API: apps, Claude Skills, Claude Code extensions, Cowork projects, or any real work in the Anthropic ecosystem
The process

How it works

Step 01

Apply

Send your CV (showing your major and coursework), a link to your GitHub with any Claude or AI-related projects, and evidence of English proficiency: a TOEFL score, or the school where you studied in English.

Step 02

Get certified

If your application is a good fit, TEG covers the cost of both the Claude Certified Developer and Claude Certified Architect exams. We'll also point you to Anthropic's free prep materials so you're ready going in.

Step 03

Join TEG's delivery bench

Once certified, you're eligible for paid contractor work: either on TEG's own client projects, or placed via staff augmentation directly with a client team, depending on fit and availability.

Benefits

What you get

Exam fees covered

TEG covers both the Claude Certified Developer and Claude Certified Architect exams as part of your onboarding.

Direct mentorship

You work alongside a team that's actively building with Claude for clients, not learning from videos alone.

A credential that travels

The Claude Certified Developer and Architect certifications are yours to keep, and your portfolio is yours regardless of what comes next.

A path to ongoing work

You'll be eligible for paid contractor work, whether on TEG's own client projects or placed with a client team via staff augmentation, depending on fit and availability.

Work with a US team

Direct communication with English-speaking leadership, valuable for any international career path.
