Our real estate roots

We started in commercial real estate.

Jim Sullivan founded The Endurance Group in 1999, after beginning his career at Arthur Andersen with a focus on commercial real estate. He learned the business from the inside: scattered property data, manual follow-up, and work that should run automatically but never does.

Those are the problems we now solve with Claude, in commercial and residential real estate alike. After real estate, Jim spent two decades running outsourced B2B sales and marketing for clients, which is why we put revenue and relationships first. He spoke at CREtech New York on why AI for real estate only works when it is connected to every system you use.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with residential as well as commercial?

Yes. Offerpad and Realty ONE Group are residential, and the agent setup covers listings, follow-up, marketing and recruiting. Our roots are in commercial real estate, and we work across both.

Do you only work with Claude?

We are a Claude Certified Services Partner, so Claude is where we go deepest. We also build ChatGPT skills and meet you where you are.

Is our client data safe?

The policy and permissions come first. Your AI workspace mirrors your existing access rules, so people only see what they should.

How do you handle independent contractor agents?

We pilot with volunteers and let adoption earn its place. A clear firm-wide policy matters most when brokers are 1099.

How much does it cost?

The assessment is free. For individual agents the setup is a fixed $998. For firms we agree the expected value first, then a fixed build price, with Managed Claude from $1,500 a month if you want us to keep it running.
