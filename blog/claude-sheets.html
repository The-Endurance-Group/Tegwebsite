Claude Sheets: What It Is, What It Can't Do Yet, and Who Should Use It

What is Claude Sheets?

Claude Sheets is a spreadsheet inside Claude where you describe what you need and Claude builds it. Reports from the launch give two examples: a campaign tracker and a hiring budget. Claude sets up the file, and your team keeps editing the same sheet.

A sheet can hold multiple tabs, formulas, standard formatting and drop-down menus. Anyone with edit access can change it, and Claude lists each cell it changes so coworkers can review the edits. Anthropic announced it at an event in San Francisco alongside Claude Whiteboards and Claude Meetings, according to TechCrunch.

Who can use Claude Sheets, and what does it cost?

Claude Sheets is in beta on all paid Claude plans, across web, desktop and mobile. Anthropic announced no new prices, so it comes with the subscription you already have.

  • Team plans: on by default.
  • Enterprise plans: off by default. An admin can turn it on in the Artifacts section of Organization settings, according to Unite.AI.
  • Free plan: not included. The coverage lists paid plans only.

What can Claude Sheets do?

Claude Sheets builds a working spreadsheet from a request and then edits it with you. In practice that covers four jobs.

  1. Build from a prompt. Ask for a tracker, budget or schedule and get tabs, formulas and drop-downs set up.
  2. Edit alongside your team. Claude changes cells directly and outlines each change.
  3. Share. The finished sheet can be shared with a team, and anyone with edit access can change it.
  4. Export. Files export to .xlsx or CSV. Anthropic also named Airtable, Asana, monday.com and Smartsheet as handoff partners.

Engadget notes it also works with Anthropic's Google Workspace plugin. For how that connection fits in, see our post on how to connect Claude to Gmail.

What can't Claude Sheets do yet?

Claude Sheets is not a replacement for Excel. Anthropic describes the spreadsheet functionality as "simple, with more in development" and sends people who need the full Excel feature set to the existing Claude for Excel add-in.

A Bloomberg interview with Anthropic product leader Nicholas Lin, reported by MLQ, adds that it is not built for sophisticated financial modeling or advanced enterprise workloads. Lin said Anthropic expects its integrations with existing spreadsheet software to handle those jobs.

How does Claude Sheets compare with Excel and Google Sheets?

Claude Sheets sits at the start of the spreadsheet workflow. Excel and Google Sheets still own the long middle, where files grow large and many people depend on them.

What else did Anthropic launch with Claude Sheets?

Claude Sheets arrived as part of a bigger push to make Claude the place where team work happens. We covered the earlier step in our post on Claude Dashboards, Motion, Docs, Slides and Design.

  • Claude Whiteboards (beta): a shared canvas where Claude turns a discussion into timelines, owners and dates, and can convert a board into a document or slide deck.
  • Claude Meetings (beta): Claude joins Google Meet calls as a named participant on Team and Enterprise plans. It leaves if an outside guest joins unless an admin allows guests.
  • Claude Data: Claude Dashboards is now generally available under this name. Certified queries and live sharing remain in beta.

Should your business use Claude Sheets?

Yes, for low-risk work where speed matters more than depth. No, for anything where a formula error would cost real money. A practical test:

  • Try it now: campaign trackers, hiring plans, simple budgets, project schedules and one-off planning sheets.
  • Pilot with review: anything a client or finance will see. Have a person check every formula and the totals.
  • Keep in Excel: financial models, large datasets and files other systems read automatically.

On Enterprise plans, confirm with your admin before anyone builds on it. It is off by default, and a beta feature can change.

When should a spreadsheet become an automation?

A spreadsheet should become an automation when people feed it by hand or when the next step depends on what is in it. Claude Sheets makes the sheet easier to build. It does not fill the sheet, keep it current or act on it.

Most teams we work with have the same pattern. A tracker starts as a good idea, then someone spends an hour each week copying numbers from the CRM, inbox or billing system into it.
