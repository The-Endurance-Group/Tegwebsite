How to Use Claude to Find Unanswered iMessages Every Morning

Conor Sullivan · Vice President · October 19, 2026
AI Automations

Claude can review your iMessages every morning and hand you a short list of the texts you never answered.

Key takeaways
  • What it does: flags every text conversation where the other person spoke last and you have not replied.
  • Why Claude is involved: it tells a real open question from a thumbs-up, a group chat tangent or a verification code.
  • How it runs: automatically each morning, with the list sent to you by text or email.
  • What it never does: reply, delete or send anything on your behalf.

Why unanswered text messages cost you business

An unanswered text is a dropped follow-up, and texts are the one channel with no built-in way to resurface them. A client asks a question while you are driving. You read it, plan to answer later, and the message sinks under 40 newer ones. Three days on, the other person assumes you are ignoring them.

Email has search, flags and inbox rules. Text messages have none of that. For contractors, sales teams, consultants and busy owners, that gap is where revenue and goodwill quietly leak out.

How a daily iMessage review with Claude works

Once it is set up, there is nothing for you to do. It runs in three steps, once a day.

  1. It checks your recent texts each morning. It looks for conversations where the last message came from someone else and has gone unanswered past a window you pick, such as 12 hours.
  2. Claude reads each one. A plain filter would flag every "ok," every reaction and every group chat tangent. Claude keeps only real open loops, like a direct question or a request waiting on you.
  3. You get a short list. Each item names the person, quotes what they asked and says how long it has been waiting. It arrives by text or email before your day starts.

It is read-only. It reports what is waiting and you decide what to answer.

Who benefits most from an unanswered-text report

Anyone whose business runs partly on text messages. The more your clients, crew or contacts reach you by text, the more it pays off.

  • Owner-operators and field-service businesses. Contractors, clinics and property managers get customer texts all day while their hands are busy. A morning list of unanswered customer questions protects revenue directly.
  • Sales leaders and account managers. A prospect who texts "can you confirm the number?" and hears nothing for two days is a lost deal. The report catches it on day one.
  • Executives and chiefs of staff. Some businesses want a single daily "needs attention" report covering unanswered texts, email and calendar conflicts. Texts are the hardest piece to capture.

It is a poor fit if you rarely use iMessage, or if your conversations live mainly in WhatsApp or Slack.

Privacy: what to know first

Treat this like any tool that can see your private messages.

  • You decide what it sees. It only works with the conversations you allow. If some are sensitive, leave them out and limit the review to specific contacts.
  • Claude reads the texts it is given. That is how it tells a real question from noise. Only the conversations in scope are shared with Claude.
  • It is read-only by design. It does not reply, delete or send anything for you.
  • You set the rules. Choose how long a message must sit unanswered before it shows up, and how many days back the review looks.

Frequently asked questions

Can Claude read my iMessages?

Yes, with your permission. Claude reads the conversations it is given and decides which ones need a reply. It sees only what you allow.

Will Claude reply to my texts for me?

No. The daily review is read-only. It tells you what is waiting and leaves every reply to you.

How does Claude tell a real question from noise?

Claude reads the whole thread, not just the last word. It skips reactions, short acknowledgments, group chat chatter and verification codes, and flags direct questions and open requests.

How often does the report arrive?

Once a day by default, at a time you choose. You can also set how long a message must sit unanswered before it appears.

Can the same review cover email or Slack?

Yes. The same idea of collecting open conversations and sorting them with Claude works for other channels. Some businesses combine texts, email and calendar conflicts into one daily report.

The bottom line

A daily unanswered-text review is a small automation that closes one of the most common gaps in a busy day. Nothing slips for three days, and nobody waits on an answer you forgot to send.
