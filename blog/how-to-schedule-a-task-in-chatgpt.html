How to Schedule a Task in ChatGPT (and Why You Should)

Scheduled tasks let ChatGPT run a prompt for you on a timer. Here is what they are, why they save time, and how to set one up.

October 6, 2026 · The Endurance Group

What is a scheduled task in ChatGPT?

A scheduled task is a prompt that ChatGPT runs for you automatically, at a time you pick. You write it once, set the schedule, and the result arrives without you opening a chat and asking. The video above shows how to set one up. Here is why it matters and the steps.

Why scheduled tasks matter

  • Less repeat work. You stop asking for the same thing every day or week.
  • It happens on time. The result is ready when you need it, not when you remember to ask.
  • Consistent output. The same prompt runs the same way each time.
  • A step toward automation. It is the simplest way to have AI working for you in the background.

How to schedule a task in ChatGPT

  1. Decide what you want. Pick something you ask for repeatedly, like a morning briefing or a weekly summary.
  2. Write the prompt. Say exactly what to include, how long it should be and how it should be formatted.
  3. Set the schedule. Choose how often it runs and at what time.
  4. Save it. Confirm the task so it starts running on its own.
  5. Check the first result. Read what came back and tweak the prompt until it is what you wanted.

Good scheduled tasks to start with

Begin with something low risk and useful: a daily briefing on your industry, a Monday summary of what to focus on, a recurring reminder, or a regular check on a topic you follow. Once one works, you will think of more.

Tips for better results

Be specific. A vague prompt gives a vague result every single time. Include who you are and what you care about, say what to leave out, and refine after the first few runs.

Frequently asked questions

What is a scheduled task in ChatGPT?

A scheduled task runs a prompt automatically at a time you choose, such as every morning or every Monday, and delivers the result to you without you opening a new chat.

Why use scheduled tasks in ChatGPT?

They remove repeat work. A briefing, summary or checklist you would otherwise ask for by hand shows up on its own, on time, every time.

How do I schedule a task in ChatGPT?

Write the prompt you want ChatGPT to run, choose how often and at what time it should run, save the task, and check the first result. Adjust the prompt if the output isn't what you wanted.

What are good scheduled tasks to set up?

Daily news or industry briefings, a weekly summary of your priorities, recurring reminders, and regular check-ins on a topic you follow are all good starting points.

How do I make a scheduled task give better results?

Be specific in the prompt about what to include, the format, and the length. Add context about who you are and what you care about, then refine after the first few runs.
