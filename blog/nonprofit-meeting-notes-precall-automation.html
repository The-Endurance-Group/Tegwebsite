How We Turned Scattered Meeting Notes Into a Working Task System

Conor Sullivan · Vice President · September 9, 2026
AI Automations

One of our nonprofit clients runs a small, fast-moving development team. Different staff use different notetakers on their calls, some clients don't allow AI notetakers at all, and none of it consistently turned into tracked follow-up. Tasks lived in people's heads or got typed into Trello inconsistently, if at all.

The problem

With everyone using a different tool to capture meetings, there was no single place notes reliably ended up. Follow-up tasks got created when someone remembered to create them. Reps walked into calls without a clear picture of what had already been discussed or promised.

What we built

We set up a shared inbox — one address every staff member forwards their meeting recap to, from whatever notetaker they used, or a quick note in their own words if the client doesn't allow AI tools on calls. From there, three automations pick up the work:

Meeting notes to Trello

Claude reads the note, figures out which client or prospect it relates to, and finds (or creates) that organization's Trello card. It assigns tasks to the right people with due dates, and adds a drafted follow-up email for the host to review and send.

Daily Trello status check

Every day at 5pm, a summary goes out to the team covering what got done, what's still open, and what's due the next day.

Pre-call prep

Each morning, it checks the next day's calendar, pulls the relevant Trello card and history for anyone with a meeting scheduled, and emails the host a prep report before they walk in.

The result

The pre-call prep piece is live and already saving the team from digging through old notes before every call. The daily Trello review is being tuned this week to run cleanly end to end. Together, the goal is simple: nothing discussed on a call disappears, and nobody walks into a follow-up call cold.

The takeaway

Small teams don't need a new CRM to fix this kind of thing. Most of the time the fix is routing information that already exists — meeting notes, calendars, a task board — to the place it's actually needed, automatically, instead of relying on someone to remember to do it by hand.

If your team's follow-up depends on people remembering to update a board, we'd be glad to talk through how we'd approach it.

Frequently asked questions

How can AI automatically turn meeting notes into Trello tasks?

An automation monitors a shared email inbox where staff forward meeting recaps — from any notetaker, or written in their own words. Claude reads each note, identifies the client or prospect it relates to, finds or creates that organization's Trello card, assigns tasks to the right people with due dates, and adds a drafted follow-up email for the host to review and send.

What is pre-call prep automation and how does it help sales and development teams?

Pre-call prep automation checks the next day's calendar each morning, pulls the relevant task board history for anyone with a meeting scheduled, and emails the host a summary of everything discussed and promised before they walk into the call. The rep arrives prepared without having to dig through old notes manually the morning of.

How do you handle meeting notes when clients don't allow AI notetakers on calls?

A shared email inbox that accepts notes in any format solves this. AI-generated summaries, voice memos transcribed manually, or a quick paragraph typed by the rep after the call all work equally well. The automation processes whatever lands in the inbox without requiring a specific tool or format, so client restrictions don't create a separate workflow for those calls.
