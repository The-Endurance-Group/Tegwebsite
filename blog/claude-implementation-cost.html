How much does Claude implementation cost?

Jim Sullivan · President · August 27, 2026
AI Automations

Claude subscription costs

Claude for Teams is $30 per user per month, billed annually. Claude Pro is $20 per month for individuals. Claude Enterprise has custom pricing for large organizations with advanced security and admin needs. These are the base subscription costs from Anthropic directly. They cover access to Claude: the model, the interface, and the usage limits your team needs to work at volume.

Implementation is separate

The subscription gives you access to Claude. Implementation is the work of configuring Claude to actually run your workflows: building Claude Skills for your specific processes, connecting Claude to your tools via MCP servers, training your team, and making sure the system holds up in production. That work is done by a Claude Partner or your own technical team.

Most B2B teams underestimate how different these two costs are. The subscription is a commodity: Anthropic charges the same rate regardless of what you do with it. Implementation is where the value gets created or lost, and it varies enormously based on the complexity of what you're automating.

What drives implementation cost

Complexity of the workflows being automated is the biggest driver. A single Skills build for one repetitive task is cheaper than a multi-step agentic system that reasons across multiple data sources and takes actions in several tools. Number of tool integrations also matters: connecting Claude to HubSpot via an official MCP is simpler than building a custom MCP for a proprietary internal system.

The engagement model is the other major variable. A fixed-scope build is a one-time cost. Ongoing support, training and improvement is a recurring one. At TEG, builds are fixed-price based on expected value and scope, and Managed Claude, our standard ongoing service, starts at $1,500/month. And the amount of judgment and reasoning the automation needs to handle reliably affects both build time and testing time. An automation that routes simple requests is faster to build and test than one that reviews contracts and flags material issues.

What to expect from a Claude partner engagement

TEG offers a free training session for teams seeking measurable value exploring Claude implementation. It's a practical way to see what a production-grade Claude deployment looks like before you commit to anything. Details at free-training.

What you get for the price

Not just the automation itself. A Claude Partner builds the Skills and MCPs, tests them against your real data and workflows, trains your team on how to use them, and is accountable for the system holding up. That's different from a freelancer who hands you a script and moves on, or a general-purpose agency that treats Claude like any other software tool.

The ongoing accountability is what makes the price defensible. Claude's models update. Your workflows evolve. The integrations need maintenance. A Claude Partner with a retainer relationship owns that maintenance. A one-time build does not.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Claude for Teams cost?

Claude for Teams costs $30 per user per month, billed annually. This gives your team access to Claude with expanded context, usage limits designed for business work, and admin controls. It does not include the implementation work of building Skills, MCP integrations, or custom automations.

How much does Claude implementation cost for a B2B company?

TEG starts with a free AI Value Assessment. We listen to you and your team, understand your work, and estimate where Claude could free time, avoid costs or create value. Builds are separately scoped and priced on expected 12-month economic value, with a fixed price agreed before work begins. Managed Claude is the standard ongoing relationship, starting at $1,500/month. New builds are priced separately; clients pay software and API charges separately.

What's included in Claude implementation?

A proper Claude implementation includes building Claude Skills tailored to your team's actual workflows, setting up MCP servers to connect Claude to your CRM, inbox, and other tools, testing against your real data, training your team on how to use the system, and ongoing maintenance as Claude's models and your workflows evolve. Implementation is what turns a Claude subscription into a system your team actually uses every day.

Is Claude implementation worth the cost?

For B2B ops teams spending significant time on work a system should handle (lead qualification, document review, data entry, reporting), Claude implementation typically pays back within a few months. The cleaner measure is time recovered: if an automation saves your team ten hours a week and your team's time costs $100/hour, a $2,000/month implementation investment covers itself in two weeks.

Can I implement Claude without a partner?

Yes, but the results vary significantly. Teams with technical staff can configure Claude Skills and basic MCP connections themselves. The gap shows up in the judgment-heavy automations: multi-step reasoning workflows, automations that need to handle edge cases reliably, and systems that need to stay calibrated as Claude's models update. A Claude Partner has built these patterns before and knows where implementation typically breaks down.
