Case Study

One AI Partner, Every Department

How The Endurance Group has helped Offerpad's teams build and scale practical AI tools, one project at a time.

Conor Sullivan · Vice President · August 3, 2026
Real Estate
Real Estate Technology AI Implementation Multi-team Partnership

The Client

Offerpad is a national real estate technology company that buys, renovates, and resells homes at scale. Moving fast across sales, renovations, and operations means a lot of manual, repetitive work spread across a lot of teams.

The Challenge

Offerpad didn't come to TEG looking for one big AI project. They came looking for a partner who could work team by team, solving real problems as they came up.

Sales needed a better way to coach reps on call quality without a manager listening to every call live. Renovations needed a faster way to review vendor work, like pool and landscaping photos, without someone manually checking every property. Accounting needed a faster way to reconcile invoices against property records instead of doing it line by line by hand.

Different teams, different tools, same underlying problem: valuable expertise that lived in a few people's heads and didn't scale with the business.

The Approach

TEG works with Offerpad the way an embedded team would, not a vendor delivering one project and disappearing.

Each new initiative starts the same way: a working session with the team closest to the problem, a scoped estimate, and a small prototype before anything bigger gets built. Projects move forward in stages, tested against real workflows before they're expanded or handed off.

Over time, that approach has covered a genuine range of work: an AI call-scoring coach for the sales team, a renovation operations platform for reviewing vendor work and invoices, and automated reconciliation tools for accounting.

The Solution

Each tool TEG has built for Offerpad follows the same principle: take a process that depends on one person's judgment and turn it into something a team can rely on consistently.

Sales

Call-Coaching Tool

Scores calls against Offerpad's own scripts, flags what reps did well or missed, so managers spend their time coaching instead of listening to recordings.

Renovations

Vendor Review Platform

Reviews service photos from pool and landscaping vendors, checks invoices against expected costs, and gives the team a clear view of what's happening property by property.

Accounting

Invoice Reconciliation

Matches invoice line items against property records using address as the key, cutting out hours of manual cross-checking each week.

Each tool was built, tested, and adjusted directly with the team that uses it every day.

The Result

"TEG has been a great AI partner for us. They helped us build a suite of AI tools for our sales team and develop back-office automations that eliminated hours of manual work. They've worked across multiple teams and delivered consistently; they know how to scope, build, and iterate without a lot of hand-holding. Highly recommend."

Chris Carpenter, Chief Operating Officer, Offerpad

The partnership has continued to grow, with new teams and new use cases added as they come up.
