What is Composio?

Composio is an integration platform built for AI agents. It gives Claude secure, authenticated access to hundreds of business apps, and the social platforms are some of the most useful: LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, and YouTube. The hard parts of connecting an AI to these platforms are sign-in, permissions, token refresh, and each platform's API quirks. Composio handles all of that and hands Claude a clean set of tools like "create a LinkedIn post" or "publish a tweet."

It plugs into Claude through MCP, the same open standard Claude uses to connect to your CRM, calendar, and documents. If you've read our piece on what MCP servers mean for your business, this is the same idea pointed at your marketing channels. It's also the same tool we use to connect multiple Gmail and CRM accounts to Claude. Once it's connected, posting is just one more thing Claude can do when you ask.

How does Claude post to social media with Composio?

When this is set up properly, a typical session goes like this:

  1. You bring the raw material. A client win, notes from a webinar, a blog post you just published, a stat that caught your eye. A rough voice memo transcript works fine.
  2. Claude drafts for each platform. A longer, story-driven version for LinkedIn, a tight version for X, and a more casual one for Facebook. Each one follows your brand voice and formatting rules, because those live in a Skill Claude loads every time.
  3. You review and edit in the chat. "Make the LinkedIn one shorter." "Drop the hashtags on X." "Tag the client's company page." You go back and forth until it sounds right.
  4. You approve, and Claude publishes. Claude calls Composio, and the posts go live on each account. You get the links back right in the conversation.

What used to take an afternoon of switching tabs now takes about ten minutes. More importantly, it's easy enough that it actually happens every week.

What mistakes do teams make when automating social posting?

The technology is the easy part. We see the same three mistakes again and again.

  • Letting Claude post without review. It's tempting to fully automate it. Don't. One off-tone post on a company page costs more than a year of saved time is worth. Every workflow we build keeps a person approving each post before it goes live.
  • Skipping the brand voice work. Without a defined voice, every post sounds like generic AI content, and your audience can tell. The Skill that holds your voice, your banned phrases, and your formatting rules is what makes the posts sound like you.
  • Treating posting as the strategy. Publishing faster doesn't help if you're publishing the wrong things. Composio solves the last mile. It doesn't decide what's worth saying, who it's for, or how it connects to your pipeline.

A few platform details are worth knowing too. Instagram requires a business or creator account and an image or video with every post. LinkedIn treats personal profiles and company pages differently. Every platform has rate limits and content rules. None of these are dealbreakers, but they're the kind of thing that turns a quick setup into a frustrating weekend if you haven't done it before.

Frequently asked questions

What is Composio?

Composio is an integration platform that gives AI agents like Claude secure, authenticated access to hundreds of business apps, including LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, and Reddit. It handles the sign-in and permissions for each app and exposes them to Claude as tools, typically through an MCP connection.

Can Claude post to LinkedIn on its own?

Not out of the box. Claude needs a connection to LinkedIn's API to publish anything. Composio provides that connection, and you can link several accounts at once, as covered in our guide to connecting multiple accounts to Claude.
