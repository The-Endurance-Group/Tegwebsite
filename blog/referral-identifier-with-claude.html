Why referral asks don't happen

For most relationship-driven businesses, referrals are the best source of new work, and almost nobody asks on a schedule. The reason is not reluctance. It's that nobody has time to review months of email and notes to decide who to ask, when, and what to say. So the ask waits for a good moment that never gets scheduled.

What Referral Identifier does

  1. Reviews your email history and CRM. Through the connectors you already use, such as Gmail and your CRM, Claude looks at how each client relationship has actually gone.
  2. Surfaces referral-ready clients. It flags clients who are overdue for a referral ask, including ones you might never have thought to ask.
  3. Drafts a personal message for each. Every draft reflects the real history with that client, not a template.
  4. Leaves the decision to you. You review the list and the drafts, then decide who to ask and when.

Who uses it

  • Professional services firms, agencies, and consultants who live on referrals.
  • Founders and account managers who know they should ask but never get to it.

What to know

The skill runs inside your own Claude account, so your email and CRM data stay with the tools you already use. Its suggestions are only as good as the history it can see, so it works best where your email and CRM are reasonably up to date.
