What Claude Skills are, and why we build inside Claude instead of a new app

Maria Jose Pava · Director of AI Solutions and Delivery · June 23, 2026
AI Automations

Claude Skills are folders of instructions, reference material, and small scripts that Claude can pull from in the middle of a conversation. Anthropic ships some by default, and a business can build its own and load it straight into Claude.ai, Claude Code, or the API. For a B2B team, that means you can teach Claude your process once, and it shows up correctly every time someone asks for it, instead of re-explaining context in every chat.

TEG is an official member of the Anthropic Claude Partner Network with certified Claude engineers on staff. We build Claude Skills, MCPs, and Projects for B2B ops teams. See our full Claude services.

How a Skill is different from a one-off prompt

A good prompt gets you one good answer. A Skill gets you the same correct process every time, from anyone on the team, without them having to remember the right prompt. It lives inside Claude itself, gets pulled in automatically when it's relevant to what someone's asking, and can reference your actual documents, your pricing logic, your tone, your exceptions, instead of relying on whatever the person typing happened to include that day.

Why we build Skills instead of a custom app

We could build almost any workflow as a standalone tool with its own login screen and dashboard. We don't, when a Skill will do the job, because of where your team already works. Your reps already have Claude open. Your ops lead already drafts in Claude. The moment we ask someone to learn a new app, remember a new password, or work inside a UI we designed instead of one they chose, adoption drops. A Skill skips all of that: we write it once, install it inside the Claude your team already has, and the workflow shows up exactly where people already are. No new interface to learn, no UI/UX to design, no app to onboard anyone onto. If you want the broader framework for what separates a real automation from a demo, we cover that here.

What a Skill can actually do for a B2B team

A lead-qualification Skill that scores a new prospect against your actual ICP, not a generic checklist. A proposal-drafting Skill that follows your firm's exact format, language, and pricing tiers instead of a blank-page draft. A CRM-hygiene Skill that knows your real pipeline stages and flags records that don't match them. Each one is narrow on purpose: it does one job, the way your team actually does it.

How does TEG build a Claude Skill?

It starts with a scoping call to understand the exact process you want Claude to follow, including the parts that aren't written down anywhere yet. We draft the Skill, install it, and test it with your team before calling it done, then refine it based on what actually comes up in use. Your first one is free, the same as any other automation, so you can see it working before any money changes hands. Frequently asked questions

Do we need to already use Claude for this to work?

No, but it helps. If your team isn't on Claude yet, we'll help you decide whether it's the right fit before we build anything, rather than building a Skill for a tool no one opens.

Is a Claude Skill the same as a custom GPT or a Gemini Gem?

Same idea, different platform. A Skill is Anthropic's version, a custom GPT is OpenAI's, and a Gem is Google's. We build whichever one matches the tool your team already has open every day.

Can a Claude Skill see our internal data?

Only what we scope it to reach. We define exactly what reference material and systems each Skill can use during the build, the same as any automation we ship.
