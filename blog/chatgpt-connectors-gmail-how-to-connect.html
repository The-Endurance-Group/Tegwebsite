ChatGPT Connectors and Plugins: How to Connect Gmail and What You Can Do With It

Connectors let ChatGPT work with apps like Gmail. Here is how to connect one, what you can ask ChatGPT to do once it is linked, and what to watch for.

October 6, 2026 · The Endurance Group

What are ChatGPT connectors and plugins?

By default, ChatGPT only knows what you type into it. Connectors and plugins change that. They link ChatGPT to the apps where your work already lives, like Gmail, so it can look things up and work with them when you ask. The video above shows how to connect one. The steps and the best uses are below.

How to connect Gmail to ChatGPT

  1. Open plugins or connectors. Find the option in ChatGPT's menu and browse the available apps.
  2. Choose Gmail. Select it from the list and start the connection.
  3. Sign in with Google. Use the account whose email you want ChatGPT to see.
  4. Approve access. Read the permissions Google shows you and approve only what you are comfortable with.
  5. Ask a question. Start a chat and ask about your email. ChatGPT now has a way to look.

What you can do once Gmail is connected

  • Find things fast. Ask for the last message from a client, or the email with a specific attachment.
  • Summarize long threads. Get the short version of a 30-message back-and-forth.
  • Pull out action items. Ask for every date, deadline and request in a conversation.
  • Draft replies with context. Have ChatGPT write a response that reflects what was already said.

Beyond Gmail

Gmail is the most common starting point, but the same pattern works for other connected apps: choose the app, sign in, approve access. The more of your work ChatGPT can see, the less copying and pasting you do.

Before you connect a work account

Connecting an app gives ChatGPT access to real business information. Read the permissions before you approve them, connect only what you need, and check your company's AI policy first if this is a work mailbox. You can disconnect at any time.

Frequently asked questions

What are ChatGPT connectors and plugins?

Connectors and plugins link ChatGPT to other apps you use, such as Gmail, so ChatGPT can read or work with that information when you ask, instead of you copying and pasting it into the chat.

How do I connect Gmail to ChatGPT?

Open the plugins or connectors option in ChatGPT, choose Gmail, sign in with your Google account, and approve the access it asks for. After that, you can ask ChatGPT questions about your email.

What can ChatGPT do with Gmail connected?

You can ask it to find a message, summarize a long thread, pull out dates and action items, or help draft a reply based on an earlier conversation.

Is it safe to connect my email to ChatGPT?

You choose what to connect and can disconnect at any time. Review the permissions before you approve them, and check your company's AI policy first if the mailbox is a work account.

Can I connect apps other than Gmail?

Yes. ChatGPT offers connectors for other common work apps, such as calendars and file storage. The setup follows the same pattern: choose the app, sign in, approve access.
